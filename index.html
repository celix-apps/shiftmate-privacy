1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Celix Apps ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the ShiftMate mobile application ("App") available on the Apple App Store. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Policy.

If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, you must immediately stop using the App.

This Policy applies solely to the ShiftMate application and does not apply to any third-party services, websites, or applications that may be linked to or integrated with the App, including but not limited to Apple Inc., iCloud, HealthKit, or the App Store. We expressly disclaim any responsibility for the privacy practices of such third parties.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Stored

2.1 Shift and Schedule Data

You may enter shift schedules, work patterns, notes, and related calendar information into the App. This data:

  • Is stored locally on your device by default.
  • Is optionally synced to your personal iCloud account if you enable iCloud Sync. When synced, data is stored in a private CloudKit container associated exclusively with your Apple ID. We do not have access to, and cannot retrieve, your iCloud data.
  • Is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party server operated by us.

You are solely responsible for the accuracy, completeness, and legality of the shift data you enter into the App.

2.2 Health Data (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, the App may request access to health-related data through Apple's HealthKit framework, including sleep analysis and physical activity data. Regarding this data:

  • It is accessed solely to display personalized insights within the App (e.g., fatigue score, sleep overview).
  • It is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any external server.
  • It is never shared with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • You may revoke HealthKit access at any time through iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → ShiftMate.
  • We do not store, retain, or cache HealthKit data outside of what is temporarily held in device memory for display purposes.

We do not use HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other than the in-app features you explicitly enable.

2.3 Diagnostic and Usage Logs

The App uses Apple's unified logging system (OSLog) to record anonymous diagnostic events (e.g., "shift added," "alarm scheduled") for the purpose of debugging and improving app stability. These logs:

  • Are stored only in your device's local system log.
  • Are never transmitted to our servers or any external analytics platform.
  • Do not contain personal identifiers, health data, or shift content.
  • May be included in a diagnostic report if you voluntarily submit feedback through Apple's crash reporting mechanism, in which case Apple's privacy policy governs that submission.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics SDK (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar). No behavioral tracking or user profiling is performed.

2.5 Subscription and Payment Data

The App offers optional Pro subscriptions processed entirely through Apple's in-app purchase system (StoreKit). Regarding payment data:

  • We do not collect, process, store, or have access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.
  • Subscription status (active/inactive) may be verified locally via StoreKit receipts.
  • All billing, refunds, and subscription management are governed exclusively by Apple's Terms of Service and Apple's privacy policy.
  • You may manage or cancel your subscription at any time through iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.

2.6 Backup and Export Data

The App may allow you to create local backups or export your shift data. Exported files:

  • Are stored in a location you choose (e.g., Files app, iCloud Drive).
  • Are your sole responsibility once exported.
  • Are not transmitted to us.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

To be unambiguous, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information (unless you contact us voluntarily).
  • Government-issued identification numbers.
  • Precise GPS location history.
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data.
  • Device identifiers for advertising purposes (IDFA, IDFV for advertising).
  • Biometric data.
  • Financial or banking information.
  • Social media profile data.
  • Any data from children under the age of 13.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information within the App solely to:

  • Provide core app functionality (shift scheduling, alarm notifications, earnings calculation, health insights).
  • Sync your data across your own devices via your personal iCloud account (if enabled).
  • Display relevant in-app notifications and alarms on your device.
  • Diagnose crashes or bugs through on-device logs.

We do not use your information for:

  • Advertising or marketing to you.
  • Selling, renting, or licensing data to third parties.
  • Profiling, scoring, or automated decision-making with legal or significant effects.
  • Training machine learning models on your personal data.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Local encryption: Data stored on your device is protected by iOS device encryption.
  • iCloud encryption: Data synced via CloudKit benefits from Apple's encryption in transit and at rest.
  • No server-side storage: Because we do not operate servers that receive your personal data, server-side breaches of your personal data by us are structurally impossible.
No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. You assume all risk associated with the use of the App, including but not limited to data loss resulting from device theft, loss, hardware failure, software bugs, iOS updates, or unauthorized access to your device. We expressly disclaim liability for any unauthorized access to or disclosure of your information that results from circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

7. Data Retention

7.1 Local Data

Data you store in the App remains on your device until you delete it within the App or uninstall the App. We do not control the retention of data stored on your device.

7.2 iCloud Data

If iCloud Sync is enabled, data in your private CloudKit container is subject to Apple's iCloud storage and retention policies. Deleting your iCloud account or disabling iCloud Sync may result in data loss. We are not responsible for data loss caused by Apple's iCloud service.

7.3 Account Deletion

If you use the "Delete Account" feature within the App, locally stored data and iCloud-synced data associated with the App will be deleted. However:

  • We cannot guarantee deletion of data cached by iOS or iCloud infrastructure outside our control.
  • Residual data in system logs may persist until the operating system purges them automatically.
  • Backup copies you have manually created (e.g., exported files) are not deleted and remain your responsibility.

16. Regional Privacy Notices

16.1 European Union & European Economic Area — GDPR

If you are located in the EU or EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to our processing of your personal data.

Data Controller: Celix Apps is the data controller for any personal data you voluntarily provide to us (e.g., support emails). For data stored on your device or in your personal iCloud account, Apple Inc. acts as a separate data controller under its own privacy policy.

Lawful Basis for Processing:

  • Consent — When you grant permissions for HealthKit, Location, or Notifications, processing is based on your explicit consent. You may withdraw consent at any time via iOS Settings.
  • Legitimate Interests — On-device diagnostic logging (OSLog) is based on our legitimate interest in maintaining app stability. This does not override your fundamental rights.
  • Contract — Processing necessary to deliver the core features you requested when you downloaded the App.

Data Transfers: We do not transfer your personal data outside your device or your personal iCloud account. iCloud data transfers are governed by Apple's Data Processing Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

Supervisory Authority: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your EU/EEA member state. A full list of authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu.

16.2 United Kingdom — UK GDPR

If you are located in the United Kingdom, the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 apply. Your rights and our obligations mirror those described in the EU GDPR section above. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

16.3 California, United States — CCPA / CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grants you the following rights:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of personal information we hold. Use the "Delete Account" feature in the App or contact us.
  • Right to Correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell, share, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. No opt-out is required.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights.

To exercise your rights, contact us at support.shiftmate@icloud.com. We will respond within 45 days as required by law.

16.4 Brazil — LGPD

If you are located in Brazil, the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018) applies. You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Confirmation of the existence of processing and access to your data.
  • Correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data.
  • Anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data.
  • Data portability to another service provider.
  • Deletion of personal data processed with your consent.
  • Information about third parties with whom data is shared.
  • Information about the possibility of denying consent and the consequences thereof.
  • Revocation of consent at any time.

You may also file a complaint with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) at gov.br/anpd.

16.5 Canada — PIPEDA

If you are located in Canada, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws apply. You have the right to access personal information we hold about you and to challenge its accuracy. You may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

16.6 Australia — Privacy Act

If you are located in Australia, the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) apply. You may request access to or correction of your personal information, and you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

16.7 Turkey — KVKK
