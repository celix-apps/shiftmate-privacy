ShiftMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

ShiftMate ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

ShiftMate stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit personal data to external servers.

  • Shift data: Stored locally and optionally synced via your personal iCloud account
  • Health data: Accessed via HealthKit with your permission; never leaves your device
  • Location data: Used only for weather alerts; not stored or shared
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data to improve the app (no personal identifiers)

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your shift data is stored in your personal iCloud account under your control. We cannot access your iCloud data.

HealthKit

ShiftMate requests HealthKit access only to display sleep and activity insights. This data is never shared with third parties.

Notifications

Notifications are processed entirely on-device. No notification content is transmitted to our servers.

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We do not store payment information.

Children

ShiftMate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
